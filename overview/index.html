Overview

Start here: what this site is, and a brief history of the case.

This site collects documentation about the Ort case — a long-running family and financial dispute in Lakewood, New Jersey — and presents the side of the story that its authors say has not been heard until now.

If you are new, read these two pages first:

  • Why this site exists — what the site is for, and what it is not for.
  • A brief history of the Ort case — the timeline in short.

From there, the Financial Overview summarizes the money claims with links to the underlying documents, and the Evidence Archive holds the cataloged scans themselves.

A note on method: every factual claim on this site should be traceable to a document. Where a scan is hard to read, undated, or ambiguous, the catalog says so. Documents are presented as the site’s authors received them; the archive does not claim to be complete.
