Evidence Archive

Stable storage and metadata for checks, scans, letters, property documents, court documents, and tables.

Evidence files live under static/evidence/<category>/<source>/<date>/, with original/, display/, and thumb/ copies side by side. Originals are never overwritten; display copies are resized for the web. Every item below records its source, date (or why the date is unknown), a transcription of what the document shows, and an honest quality label.

Metadata Format Proposal

Use data/evidence.yml as the central, diff-friendly evidence catalog. Narrative pages keep their own front matter, while evidence records stay in structured data.

Required fields:

  • id
  • title
  • date
  • source_category
  • source_name
  • related_pages
  • amount
  • quality_status
  • transcription
  • original_path
  • display_path
  • notes

Recommended quality labels:

  • good
  • usable
  • poor
  • unrecoverable

Recommended path convention for checks:

static/evidence/checks/<source>/<yyyy-mm-dd>/<original|display|thumb>/<files>

If the source or date is unknown, use a conservative placeholder such as unknown/undated and update the metadata later after manual review.
