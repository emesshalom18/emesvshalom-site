Rabbonim Letters

The rabbinical letters and writs on the Ort case, transcribed in full.

The site’s collection of letters from the rabbonim involved with the case over the years, transcribed in full from the original site. Each letter page links to the scanned original in the Evidence Archive .

Writs concerning Mrs. Ort

  • KSAV SIRUV — summoning Mrs. Manya Ort to din Torah; Isru Chag Shavuos 5762. Signed by R. Yakov Hopfer, R. Simcha Bunim Shafrin, R. Mordechai Shuchatowitz. ( scan )
  • Siruv from the Beis Yosef Beis Din ( scan )
  • The Ikul — injunction against selling the Lakewood house; 2 Menachem Av 5768. ( scan )
  • Bais Din ruling on mesira — that aside from the issur of going to arkaos, the graver issur of mesira was committed; 5763. ( scan )
  • Siruv against Miriam Ort-Hirsch — 13 Shevat 5763. ( scan )
