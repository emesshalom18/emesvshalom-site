The Manhattan co-op

The co-op is the newest item on the list: a Manhattan apartment the court awarded to Mrs. Ort, which she then sold.

  • The Seward Park stock certificate (#3556, 22.75 shares, Unit E504) was issued in Mrs. Ort’s name in September 2006.
  • Per the closing letter of January 15, 2013 (Re: Ort vs. Ort, Docket No. FM-15-990-00N), the apartment at 415 Grand Street sold for $445,000 plus a $308.52 credit — $445,308.52 gross. After closing debits the adjusted net was $299,212.04 (the figure used in the overview table); after arbitration and renovation payments, Mrs. Ort’s net proceeds were $239,664.25.

The $600,000

In January 2000 Mrs. Ort wired $350,000 from her late mother-in-law’s Discount Bank account to an Israel account already holding $250,000, then gave the full $600,000 to her cousin R’ Ira Adler to disburse to “a yeshiva” (unnamed). The cataloged documents:

  • Her handwritten authorization for the $350,000 wire, dated January 4, 2000.
  • Her deposition excerpt confirming the $600,000 total and stating it was donated “to a yeshiva.”
