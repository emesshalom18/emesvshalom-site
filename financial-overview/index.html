Reading the numbers honestly

  • The support checks vs. the $342,210 claim. The 47 newly cataloged support checks total $75,075 and are dated January 2005–November 2007 — outside the claimed June 2000–August 2001 window for the $342,000. The table keeps the site’s figure; the scans are linked so readers can see what they actually show.
  • The $6,000,000 commercial-building sale. The two 2017 settlement agreements now cataloged each carry a $3,000,000 settlement sum — Jamaica Avenue (Queens action) and East 16th Street (Kings County action) — which together match the site’s $6,000,000 figure. Both settlements are cross-conditioned and expressly exclude Abraham Ort from the releases.
  • The co-op row. The $299,212 figure is the adjusted net from the closing, not the sale price. Per the attorney’s closing letter , the apartment sold for $445,000 plus a $308.52 credit ($445,308.52 gross); after the flip tax, maintenance arrears, commissions, and closing costs the adjusted net was $299,212.04, and after further arbitration and renovation payments Mrs. Ort’s net proceeds were $239,664.25. The table uses the adjusted-net figure.
  • The 2006 yearly subtotal. In the original site’s year-by-year breakdown, the 2006 row lists components of $132,000 + $50,000 + $52,333 but prints a row total of $243,333 — a $9,000 addition slip. The components sum to $234,333, and the grand total of $11,175,850 is consistent with the corrected figure. This page uses $234,333 for 2006.
  • What “received” means here. These are the original site’s categories and figures: money it attributes to Mrs. Ort across fourteen years. Some rows (child support, custodial accounts) describe money paid out or held for the children rather than money Mrs. Ort kept. The categories are presented as the site presents them; the evidence links let readers check each one.
