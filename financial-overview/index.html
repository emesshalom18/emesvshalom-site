Reading the numbers honestly

  • The $342,210 reconciliation row. The 2000–2001 figure is documented by the arbitration excerpt : $316,210.52 released in four court-ordered payments (April 2000–April 2001), plus the $26,000 Fleet check of June 2000 shown on the same page — together $342,210.52, matching the row. (The case history describes this as “over $300,000” released by consent during the reconciliation attempt; the $316,210.52 in documented releases plus the $26,000 check reconcile to the table figure.) The 47 newly cataloged support checks ($75,075, January 2005–November 2007) are a separate, later set; see the day-to-day support page .
  • The $6,000,000 commercial-building sale. The two 2017 settlement agreements now cataloged each carry a $3,000,000 settlement sum — Jamaica Avenue (Queens action) and East 16th Street (Kings County action) — which together match the site’s $6,000,000 figure. Both settlements are cross-conditioned and expressly exclude Abraham Ort from the releases.
  • The co-op row. The $299,212 figure is the adjusted net from the closing, not the sale price. Per the attorney’s closing letter , the apartment sold for $445,000 plus a $308.52 credit ($445,308.52 gross); after the flip tax, maintenance arrears, commissions, and closing costs the adjusted net was $299,212.04, and after further arbitration and renovation payments Mrs. Ort’s net proceeds were $239,664.25. The table uses the adjusted-net figure.
  • The 2006 yearly subtotal. The year-by-year breakdown lists 2006 components of $132,000 + $50,000 + $52,333 but prints a row total of $243,333 — a $9,000 addition slip. The components sum to $234,333, and the grand total of $11,175,850 is consistent with the corrected figure. This page uses $234,333 for 2006.
  • What “received” means here. These are the case’s categories and figures: money attributed to Mrs. Ort across fourteen years. Some rows (child support, custodial accounts) describe money paid out or held for the children rather than money Mrs. Ort kept. The categories are presented as documented; the evidence links let readers check each one.
